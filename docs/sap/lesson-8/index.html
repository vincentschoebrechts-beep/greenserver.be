SAP from the shop floor · Lesson 8 of 10

Plant maintenance

How SAP PM describes the installations, records breakdowns, plans repairs and triggers preventive maintenance, including from machine counters.

In this lesson

  • Tell functional locations and equipment apart.
  • Follow a breakdown from notification to order.
  • Understand counter-based preventive maintenance.

Technical objects

A functional location is a place in the technical structure of the plant, organised as a tree: site, line, station. An equipment is an individual physical object, such as a pump or a motor, with its own serial number and history. It is installed at a functional location and can be moved to another one, taking its history with it.

Notification and order

  1. Notification (IW21): someone reports a need. In the SAP standard, M1 is a maintenance request, M2 a malfunction report, M3 an activity report.
  2. Maintenance order (IW31): the work is planned, with operations, spare parts and external services. The order collects confirmations of time and the actual costs.
  3. Completion: the technician confirms the work, records the damage and the cause, and the order is closed.

Preventive maintenance

Maintenance plans create orders or notifications automatically:

  • Time-based: every three months, every year.
  • Performance-based (counter-based): every 10,000 cycles, every 500 operating hours.

Counters are measuring points on the equipment. Each reading creates a measurement document; when the counter reaches the next due value, the plan generates the work.

A natural job for industrial IT

PLCs and the supervision already count cycles and operating hours. Sending these counters to SAP as measurement documents, through the MES or an interface, replaces manual readings and lets maintenance follow the real use of each machine.

On the shop floor

The press on line 1 is functional location 1100-L1-PRESS; its hydraulic pump is an equipment with a counter of operating hours. Every night, an interface reads the hour counter from the supervision and posts a measurement document. At 2,000 hours, the maintenance plan creates an order to change the filter, with the filter already listed as a spare part.

Key points

  • Functional location: a place. Equipment: an object that can move between places.
  • Notification (IW21) to report, order (IW31) to plan, execute and collect costs.
  • Maintenance plans are time-based or counter-based.
  • Measuring points and measurement documents can be fed from PLC counters.
