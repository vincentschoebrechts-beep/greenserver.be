SAP from the shop floor · Lesson 3 of 10

Master data for production

The material master, the bill of materials, the work center, the routing and the production version: the five records production relies on.

In this lesson

  • Read a material master and know its main views.
  • Understand how BOM, routing and work center fit together.
  • Know why the production version matters in S/4HANA.

The material master

Every item has one material master record: raw material, semi-finished part, finished product, spare part. It is organised in views that each department fills in: basic data, purchasing, MRP, work scheduling, quality management, accounting, costing. Most views are maintained per plant, because the same part can be planned differently in two factories.

The material type sets which views are allowed and how stock is valued. The standard types are ROH (raw material), HALB (semi-finished), FERT (finished product), HAWA (trading goods) and ERSA (spare parts); companies often add their own.

Bill of materials, work center, routing

RecordAnswers the questionContains
Bill of materials (BOM)What is it made of?Components and quantities for a base quantity of the product
Work centerWhere is the work done?A machine, line or team, with its capacity, its working times and its cost rates
RoutingHow is it made?The sequence of operations, each at a work center, with standard times for setup, machine and labour

Each operation has a control key, which says for example whether it is scheduled, costed and must be confirmed, or whether it is done by a subcontractor.

The production version

A product may have two BOMs (two designs) or two routings (two lines). The production version says which BOM and which routing go together, for which period and which lot sizes. In S/4HANA it is mandatory for materials produced in-house: MRP finds the BOM through it. A material without a valid production version will not be planned correctly.

On the shop floor

The finished pump P-200 (type FERT) has a BOM with one motor, one housing and eight screws. Its routing has operation 10, assembly at work center ASSY1, and operation 20, test at work center TEST1. Production version 0001 links that BOM and that routing. The test bench at TEST1 is what the PLC and the supervision see; in SAP it is a work center with a capacity.

Key points

  • One material master per item, in views, mostly per plant.
  • BOM: what it is made of. Routing: how. Work center: where.
  • The control key decides how each operation is handled.
  • In S/4HANA, no production version means no correct planning.
