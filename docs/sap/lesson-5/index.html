SAP from the shop floor · Lesson 5 of 10

The production order

The life of a production order from creation to closing, its statuses, and the goods movements and confirmations that the shop floor feeds.

In this lesson

  • Follow a production order through its statuses.
  • Know when components are issued and finished goods received.
  • Understand what a confirmation reports.

Creation and release

A production order is created by converting a planned order, or by hand (CO01). SAP copies the operations of the routing and the components of the BOM, through the production version, and reserves the components. An availability check tells whether the parts are there.

The release is the moment the order may be executed: shop papers can be printed, the order can be sent to the MES, and SAP accepts goods movements and confirmations.

Statuses

StatusMeaning
CRTDCreated: planned, not yet executable
RELReleased: can be executed
PCNF / CNFPartially confirmed / confirmed
PDLV / DLVPartially delivered / delivered to stock
TECOTechnically completed: no more production expected
CLSDClosed: costs settled, nothing more can be posted

Goods issue, confirmation, goods receipt

  1. Goods issue (movement type 261): the components leave stock and are charged to the order. With backflushing, this happens automatically when the operation is confirmed.
  2. Confirmation (CO11N): for each operation, the good quantity (yield), scrap, rework and the times actually spent. A confirmation can be partial or final. CO13 cancels one.
  3. Goods receipt (movement type 101): the finished products enter stock. It can be posted automatically with the confirmation of the last operation.
  4. Closing: the order is set to TECO, then its costs are settled to controlling, which shows the difference between planned and actual cost.

To follow many orders at once, COOIS lists orders, operations and components with their statuses.

On the shop floor

A counter on the assembly line counts 82 pumps for order 1000123, and the test bench rejects 2. At the end of the shift, the MES sends a confirmation for operation 20: yield 80, scrap 2, machine time 7.5 hours. SAP backflushes the screws and motors, and posts the goods receipt of 80 pumps automatically. Nobody typed anything in SAP.

Key points

  • Operations come from the routing, components from the BOM.
  • Release opens the order to confirmations and goods movements.
  • 261 issues components, 101 receives finished goods; backflushing automates the issue.
  • A confirmation reports yield, scrap and times; TECO ends production, settlement closes the costs.
