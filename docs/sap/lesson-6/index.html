SAP from the shop floor · Lesson 6 of 10

Stock and goods movements

How SAP records every change in stock: movement types, stock types, documents, and the transactions the store uses every day.

In this lesson

  • Know the most common movement types.
  • Understand the three stock types.
  • Find a material's stock and its movements.

Movement types

Every change in stock is a goods movement, and every goods movement has a three-digit movement type that tells SAP what is happening and how to post it.

TypeMovement
101Goods receipt for a purchase order or a production order
261Goods issue to an order
201Goods issue to a cost center (consumption without an order)
311Transfer between storage locations, in one step
321Release from quality inspection to unrestricted-use stock
551Withdrawal for scrapping

A movement is reversed with the matching reversal type, usually the next number: 102 reverses 101, 262 reverses 261.

Stock types

In a storage location, the same material can be in three stock types:

  • Unrestricted-use: free to be consumed or shipped.
  • Quality inspection: waiting for a quality decision.
  • Blocked: not to be used.

Documents and value

Each goods movement creates a material document. Stock has a value, so a valued movement also creates an accounting document: logistics and finance move together. In S/4HANA, stock quantities are calculated from the material documents themselves, which are stored in one table.

Daily transactions

  • MIGO: post and display most goods movements.
  • MMBE: stock overview of a material by plant, storage location and stock type.
  • Physical inventory: stock is counted and the differences are posted, so that SAP matches the shelves.

On the shop floor

The storekeeper moves 50 housings from the main store (0001) to the line-side rack (0003): movement type 311. Two housings are found dented: movement type 551, scrapping. If the line-side rack is not a separate storage location in SAP, the supervision may show a full rack while SAP shows nothing there: agreeing on storage locations early saves a lot of confusion.

Key points

  • The movement type says what happens: 101 receipt, 261 issue to order, 311 transfer, 551 scrap.
  • Mistakes are reversed, never deleted (102 for 101, 262 for 261).
  • Unrestricted-use, quality inspection, blocked: only the first is free to use.
  • MIGO posts movements, MMBE shows stock; each movement leaves a material document.
