Bike + Train

A trip planner that combines cycling and trains, in Belgium and 34 European countries.

What it can do

Pick the right stations

It works out where to get on and off the train so the cycling stays reasonable, using trains you can actually catch at your own pace.

One train, if possible

The no-change mode gives you a ride, one direct train, then another ride. Fewer platforms, less stress with a loaded bike.

Multi-stop trips

Add stops along the way, and choose bike or train for each leg by clicking on the map.

Bikes-on-trains rules

Reservations, extra fees, folding bikes only: the rules change from one country to the next, and the app tells you. Ferry crossings are included.

Made for Belgium

SNCB timetables

Belgian rail timetables are built into the app and confirmed in real time when the live service responds.

Cycle node network

Enter a sequence of numbered junctions, such as 50, 51, 52, or click them on the map: the app builds the route, including getting there by bike or train.

RAVeL greenways

Follow a RAVeL line or another greenway, end to end or only between your start and finish.

Four languages

English, French, Dutch and German.

When will it be online?

The app already works, just not publicly yet: bike routing needs a server that can keep up. It will land here, on greenserver.be.

Where the data comes from

  • Maps and bike routing: © OpenStreetMap contributors.

  • Trains across Europe: Transitous.
  • Cycle nodes and RAVeL: Tourisme Wallonie, Toerisme Vlaanderen, visit.brussels and the Public Service of Wallonia.
